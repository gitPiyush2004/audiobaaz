Five steps,
start to payout.

Built for the Indian audiophile community. Simple enough to do in 10 minutes. Solid enough to trust with serious gear.

01
Create your listing
~10 min

Walk through our guided 6-step form. Product, condition, inclusions, photos, price. Simple enough that you get it right the first time.

02
We verify it
Under 24hr

Our team reviews your listing and assigns an official condition grade. S, A, B, or C. Consistent. Non-negotiable. No surprises for buyers.

03
Buyer pays into escrow
Instant hold

When an offer is accepted, payment is locked in escrow. You never touch the money until delivery is confirmed. The buyer is protected too.

04
Ship the gear
48hr inspection

Ship it. The buyer has 48 hours to inspect. If everything checks out, or if they don't respond, escrow releases to you automatically.

05
Get paid via UPI
10% only when sold

Payment goes directly to your UPI. We take 10% only when the gear sells. No listing fee. No subscription. If it doesn't sell, you pay nothing.

The process

From listing
to payout.

Two roles. Five steps. Most of the work is ours — we verify, hold escrow, and release payment automatically.

01 · Create
Post your listing

Walk through our guided form — product, condition, inclusions, photos, price. Simple enough to get right the first time.

You · ~10 min
02 · Verify
We grade it

Our team reviews and assigns an official condition grade — S, A, B, or C. Consistent, non-negotiable, published within 24 hours.

Audiobaaz · Under 24hr

When an offer is accepted, payment locks automatically into escrow. You never chase anyone for money. The buyer is protected too.

Automatic · Instant
04 · Ship
